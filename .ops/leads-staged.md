## Why the US half may matter more under a revenue offer
Bigger average deal sizes, cold outreach is a normal B2B motion rather than an
imposition, CAN-SPAM is permissive where Finnish law is opt-out-with-conditions,
and several of these (Kernutt Stokes, APS, Fellow Health, Sustainable Billing)
are themselves revenue-cycle or growth businesses — buyers who already think in
pipeline terms and do not need the concept explained.
