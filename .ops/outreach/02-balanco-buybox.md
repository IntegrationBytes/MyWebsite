## The situation, in numbers he already knows

- Public target: **€35M revenue by 2027**, stated repeatedly since May 2024.
- Where they are: **~€18M pro forma**, ~220 staff, after Tili-Minetti (Feb 2026).
- Typical deal size: Accotilit €1.6M, Tili-Minetti €1.3M, Lüdemann ~€1M.
- So the gap is roughly **€17M ≈ ten more acquisitions in about a year.**
- Ownership: Mikko Marttunen (CEO) 16%, **Voittonen 16%**, Edith Lau 16%,
  staff 29%, Voland Partners 23%.

Their own stated bottleneck, verbatim from the Denmark release:

> *"Etsimme nyt yhtiöitä, joiden johtohenkilöt ovat valmiita rakentamaan
> kasvua kanssamme."*

They are not short of capital or appetite. They are short of **findable owners
who are ready**. That is a search problem, and it is exactly what the pipeline
does.

## Rules for this one

1. **Never say AI, agents, automation or tekoäly.** Balanco has a
   *tekoälyjohtaja* (Markus Marttunen) and has invested in automation and RPA
   for years. An AI-flavoured email gets forwarded to him and dies there. Sell
   deal flow. The method is irrelevant to the buyer.
2. **Do not pitch back-office automation.** They sell that themselves.
3. **Anchor against an M&A advisor, not against software.** An advisor takes
   3–5% of deal value; on a €1.5M transaction that is €45,000–75,000. A fixed
   €7,500 with no success fee is a rounding error, and that comparison is the
   whole argument.
4. **Attach three real candidates.** Not a description of the method — three
   named firms with owner, revenue and a succession signal, with sources. The
   sample IS the pitch. Do the work before the email, not after the yes.

## Before sending — the work that has to exist first

The three sample targets must be real, sourced and correct. **Do not send this
email without them.** They are the product; the email is just the wrapper.

Profile to search for: Finnish accounting firms (tilitoimisto), €1–3M revenue,
owner-managed, with a public succession signal — founder over ~55, a recent
board change, a partial share sale, an authorised-firm listing that has gone
quiet, or a Taloushallintoliitto membership with no recent activity. The Balanco
pattern is Tampere/Pirkanmaa and mid-sized regional firms, so weight there.

Sources that carry these signals: PRH/YTJ trade register, Taloushallintoliitto's
member directory, Asiakastieto/Profinder financials, Kauppalehti's Yritykset
listings, and local business media.
