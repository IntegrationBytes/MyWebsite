# Market check before changing the offer — verified, not assumed

Written after Vincent said: *"You may change offer, I'm also able to use AI to
make sales and marketing etc."*

The obvious move — launch an AI marketing / GEO service — is a trap, for two
independently verified reasons.

---

## 1. Finnish GEO / AI-optimointi is already a commodity

Live pricing fetched from the providers' own pages:

| Provider | Price | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Silta AI | **149 €/mo** (GEO), SEO from 199 €/mo | claims *"yli 300 yritystä luottaa meihin"*; two months free trial |
| Markkinointi Ukkonen | **from 150 €/mo** | no fixed term, 1 month notice |
| Frank (wearefrank.fi) | **390–600** starter / 800–1500 growth / 1500–2500+ full | free AI visibility report as the lead magnet |
| Netello | quote | 25 years of SEO behind it |
| Grapevine, Brandteam | quote | established agencies, full GEO service pages |

The entry price is **€149–390/month** and the free "AI visibility report" is
*already* the standard lead magnet — Frank leads with exactly that.

**Consequence:** at those prices a solo operator needs 50+ retained clients to
earn a living, and would be the least-credentialled entrant in a field of
agencies with decades of history. This is a volume business. Vincent needs a
high-ticket business. Do not build a me-too monthly GEO retainer, and do not
build the free AI-visibility-report lead magnet — that position is taken.

## 2. Wannado Marketing already sells GEO

`wannadomarketing.com/palvelut/tekoalyoptimointi/`, published **27 May 2026**:
*"Tekoälyoptimointi (GEO) tuntihintaan"*. Their full line is:

- **Digimarkkinointi** — social, paid, SEO, annual plans, reporting
- **Verkkosivut** — WordPress / Webflow / WIX, plus tracking, analytics,
  integrations and **automaatiot**, GDPR and accessibility
- **Design ja media**
- **Kansainvälistyminen** — *"10 vuoden kokemuksella kaikki viennin palvelut
  Eurooppaan tai USA:an lähteville pk-yrityksille: rahoituksen haku,
  markkinatutkimus, markkinointi, **myynti** ja business landing"*
- **Tekoälyoptimointi (GEO)** — hourly

They bill **by the hour** and position explicitly against monthly contracts:
*"Et välttämättä tarvitse kokopäiväistä työntekijää tai raskasta
kuukausisopimusta."*

**Consequence:** a Vincent-branded GEO service competes with his own family
business in a commodity market. Commercially bad, personally worse.

---

## What this actually points at

Three things follow, and together they define the offer:

**a) Sell the system, not the hours.** Every competitor here sells labour by the
month or by the hour — Wannado included. Vincent's real asymmetry is that he
*builds systems the client owns and runs in their own cloud*. That is the one
sentence a €390/month agency cannot say back.

**b) He already has product proof, generated on himself.** The overnight run in
this repo produced 32 named Finnish SMEs, each with a verified decision-maker, a
published contact route confirmed by fetching the page, and a specific dated
trigger — Balanco's 17.08.2026 acquisition where they themselves blamed a
technology shift, Yle's 27.08.2026 report of Finnglass's €12M defence
investment, Eltrea taking PE money while buying a competitor. Plus a
verification stage that caught ~25% of machine-suggested email addresses as
fabricated. **That artifact is the demo, the case study and the deliverable at
once.** He has no client proof; he has product proof. Nobody selling GEO in
Finland can show that, because it is not what they sell.

**c) Wannado is manufacturing capacity and distribution, not a rival.**
~60 assistants billing €42/h, 700+ SME clients, and a Kansainvälistyminen line
selling *sales and market research* to Finnish SMEs entering Europe and the USA.
A company expanding into a market where it knows nobody has exactly the problem
a trigger-driven outbound engine solves — and Wannado already has those
customers and already sells them sales support. Vincent's software makes their
billable hours more productive instead of replacing them; GEO demand gets routed
to Wannado rather than fought over.

## The trap to avoid, stated plainly
Launching "Vincent Viitala — AI marketing for SMEs" at €400/month would put him
in a crowded commodity market, against his own family business, with the least
proof of anyone in it, in the one business model where his single real
advantage — that he can build systems — counts for nothing.
