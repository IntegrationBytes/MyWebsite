Assumptions & methodology
  • Lift: percent increase in revenue or cost savings driven by automation.
  • Gross margin: applied to estimate additional gross profit.
  • Annualized ROI: (12 × monthly gain × margin − cost) ÷ cost.
  • Typical benchmarks: 3–10% lift in pipeline quality; ~20–40% support deflection; 20–60% time saved on internal workflows.
