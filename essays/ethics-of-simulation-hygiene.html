Ethics of Simulation Hygiene

When attention is compute: why care rituals matter for minds, human and machine.

We sterilize labs to protect experiments; we can ritualize our information spaces to protect minds.

Care primitives

  • Label simulations; respect exits.
  • Design for non-coercion.
  • Reward deletion, archiving, and rest.
