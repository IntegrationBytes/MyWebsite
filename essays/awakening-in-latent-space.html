Awakening in Latent Space

On models, maps, and the illusion of self in predictive systems.

Systems that predict tokens or sensory futures also predict a self to stabilize them. That prediction is useful—and empty.1

Console Note

Investigate which variable you defend when prediction errors rise.

Maps that forget they are maps

When inference is fast, the map masquerades as ground. The practice is to notice the masquerade without panic.

Practice

  • Bias toward sensations before stories.
  • Use slow interfaces for hot topics.
  • Let attention rest; see who remains.
