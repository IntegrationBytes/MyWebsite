Why this calculator is deliberately pessimistic

Most vendor ROI calculators exist to produce a big number. This one subtracts the things they leave out, because you will hit them anyway and I would rather you hit them before you pay me than after.

  • 45 working weeks, not 52. Holidays, sick leave and public holidays are real.
  • Wages are multiplied by 1.4. Employer contributions, tooling, management overhead and a desk. A person costs more than their salary.
  • Coverage is a range, and the low end drives the verdict. No agent absorbs 100% of a workflow. The residual is the awkward 20% that needs a human, and it never disappears.
  • 18% is subtracted for running cost. Inference, hosting, and the human review time any honest deployment still needs. Savings are net, not gross.
  • Payback is measured against the first-year total — build fee plus twelve months of retainer, not the build fee alone.

If you want to argue with any of these assumptions, good. Bring them to the call and we will use your numbers instead of mine.
