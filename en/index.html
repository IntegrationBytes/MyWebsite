Common Questions

Before you book the call

What if our data can't leave the EU?
Default deployment is EU-resident — Hetzner, AWS Frankfurt, Azure West Europe, or your own on-prem. EU-hosted models (Mistral, Llama) are used where required. The audit trail and knowledge layer stay inside your perimeter. GDPR is the baseline, not the upgrade.
What if my team isn't technical?
Team enablement is part of the Build, not an upsell. Your people learn to brief, supervise, and improve agents — no Python required. Dashboards are written for the operator who has to use them, not the engineer who built them.
What happens if I cancel the AgentOps retainer?
The platform is yours. Cancel any month. Your agents keep running. The stack is built on open standards (MCP, LangGraph, your cloud) — any competent developer can take it over. There is no proprietary lock-in to walk away from.
Why not just hire an AI consultant or agency?
Most consultants ship slide decks; most agencies ship a pilot and disappear. I ship infrastructure you own, agents in production, and ongoing operation — with one accountable person on the other end of the phone. Fixed fee on the Build, value-pegged retainer with a refund guarantee on the first agent.
Our workflows are weird and specialized — does that break this?
The opposite. Generic chatbot demos lose to free GPT wrappers; agents that understand your specific contracts, your ERP quirks, and your tribal knowledge are defensible. Specialized workflows are exactly where the ROI lives.
How much of my time will the Build cost?
Roughly 4–6 hours/week for the first month (workflow walkthroughs, data access, stakeholder reviews), then 1–2 hours/week. I write the code; you make the decisions. Slow weeks on your side don't stop the build.
