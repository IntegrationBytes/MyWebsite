Common Questions

Before you book the call

What if our data can't leave the EU?
By default everything lives in the EU: Hetzner, AWS Frankfurt, Azure West Europe, or your own on-prem. Where the rules demand it, I run EU-hosted models like Mistral or Llama. Your audit trail and knowledge layer never leave your perimeter. GDPR isn't a paid add-on here, it's where I start.
What if my team isn't technical?
Training your team is part of the Build, not an upsell. Your people learn to brief an agent, supervise it, and make it better, none of which needs Python. The dashboards are built for whoever's running the thing day to day, no engineering background assumed.
What happens if I cancel the AgentOps retainer?
The platform is yours, full stop. Cancel any month and your agents keep running. The stack sits on open standards (MCP, LangGraph, your own cloud), so any decent developer can pick it up where I left off. Nothing proprietary is holding you hostage.
Why not just hire an AI consultant or agency?
Most consultants leave you with a slide deck. Most agencies ship one pilot, invoice, and you never hear from them again. I hand you infrastructure you own, agents running in production, and someone who stays on to operate them and answers the phone. The Build is a fixed fee. The retainer is pegged to value, with a refund guarantee on the first agent.
Our workflows are weird and specialized. Does that break this?
It's the whole point. A generic chatbot demo can't beat a free GPT wrapper. But an agent that knows your contracts, your ERP's quirks, and the things only your people know because they've done the job for years is genuinely hard to copy. The weirder the workflow, the bigger the moat.
How much of my time will the Build cost?
Figure 4 to 6 hours a week in the first month for workflow walkthroughs, data access, and stakeholder reviews. After that it drops to 1 to 2 hours. I write the code, you make the calls.
